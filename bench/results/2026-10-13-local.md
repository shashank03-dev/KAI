# Holo3.1 4B on a CPU, and the cut-off reply fix

Date: 2026-10-13. Model: `holo-3.1-4b-q4` (H Company, Q4_K_M, community GGUF,
from Kai's catalog), served by llama.cpp b11429 with Kai's lean flags
(`--reasoning off`, `-np 1`, …). Raw files:
[`2026-10-13-local/`](2026-10-13-local/).

## How it was measured

- **Machine:** this repo's Linux cloud container: 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM, **no
  GPU**. Xvfb desktop with openbox, AT-SPI, Firefox ESR and Mousepad
  (`scripts/desktop/`).
- **Tasks:** the 14 from the 2026-10-12 live Gemma runs. A task passes only
  if its `check` command passes.
- **Command:** `kai bench --backend local --model holo-3.1-4b-q4 --yes
  --no-record --only …`, one run per build:
  - **round 1:** before the cut-off reply fix (`2abea69`);
  - **round 2:** after it, plus the local server's port wait (`652175e`).

## Results

| | Passed | Model calls | Repair calls | Model p50 | Step p95 | Wall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| round 1 (before) | 8/14 | 83 | 16 | 59.2 s | 172 s | 5,933 s |
| round 2 (after) | 6/14 | 79 | **6** | 59.5 s | **118 s** | **5,035 s** |

Repair calls are counted from the log lines "repairing malformed reply".

## What the numbers say

- **The fix does what it was built for.** Repair calls fell from 16 to 6
  (−62%), step p95 from 172 s to 118 s, and wall time by 15%. In round 1,
  9 of the first 10 cut replies were cut inside `then`. Replayed offline
  through the fix, 9 of those 10 were kept; the tenth was cut before its
  `risk`, so it is correctly still repaired.
- **The pass rate is noise at this sample size:** one run per build, and
  different tasks failed in each round. `find-largest` failed only in
  round 1, while `web-form-submit`, `download-file` and `close-app` failed
  only in round 2. Gemma 4 26B passed 27–28/28 on the same tasks
  (2026-10-12).
- **A 4B model on 4 CPU cores is too slow to be pleasant:** ~60 s per model
  call, 6–11 minutes per web task. It stays `untested` in the catalog
  (not recommended). Its natural home is a GPU, where llama.cpp runs it many
  times faster; that was not measured here (no GPU).
- **Not measured:** Holo4 35B-A3B (needs ~24 GB of memory; this machine has
  16 GB), Fara 7B, Gemma 4 12B QAT and any GPU numbers.
